Infrastructure

Fiber and satellite. Internet anywhere.

Broadband over fiber to the home, plus satellite links for rural and remote zones where terrestrial infrastructure ends. Both paths land on the same core network and the same NOC.

Access architecture GPON, XGS-PON, VSAT

Fiber to the home

GPON, XGS-PON

Passive optical networks delivering ultra-fast internet to homes, buildings and businesses, with symmetric speeds from 100 Mbps to 10 Gbps.

  • GPON and XGS-PON architecture
  • Symmetric speeds up to 10 Gbps
  • Aerial or underground deployment
  • Centralized NOC with continuous monitoring
  • Integration with OSS and BSS platforms

Satellite connectivity

VSAT, LEO, GEO

Satellite internet for rural communities, remote sites and critical applications, combining GEO capacity with low-latency LEO constellations.

  • Low-latency LEO constellations
  • VSAT for remote and mission-critical sites
  • Coverage for rural and hard-to-reach communities
  • Contracted service levels with dedicated support
  • Links for government and emergency response
